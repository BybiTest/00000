* **📅 Interactive Content Calendar:** Plan, schedule, track, and manage your publishing pipeline seamlessly with offline Room database persistence.
* **🤖 AI Creator Assistant Chat:** Direct access to an elite social media strategist trained to analyze your scripts, audit retention curves, and scale your channel.

## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Assets

### Visual Concept
* **Concept:** Dynamic creator flow curve fused with an inspiration spark and abstract C/F monogram in electric violet, cobalt, and radiant gold.
* **Style:** High-end 2026 mobile app design, clean vector geometry, smooth 3D lighting, ultra-sharp resolution.

### Brand Color Palette
* **Obsidian Dark Canvas (`#090A0F`):** Deep, distraction-free creative workspace.
* **Electric AI Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** Creative intuition, viral hooks, and AI intelligence.
* **Creator Flow Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Video production, execution, and broadcast quality.
* **Radiant Gold (`#F59E0B`):** VIP Unlimited membership, high-retention metrics, and quality badges.
* **Glassmorphic Slate (`#131622`):** Elevated card surfaces and navigation controls.

### App Visual Assets Generated
* **1. Master Launcher Icon:** 1024x1024 master icon (`/public/icon.jpg`).
* **2. Android Adaptive Icon:** Foreground vector layer (`ic_launcher_foreground.xml`) and background layer (`ic_launcher_background.xml`) supporting circle, squircle, and rounded-corner masks.
* **3. Splash Screen Design:** 9:16 mobile wallpaper (`/public/splash.jpg`).
* **4. Brand Color System:** Defined in Compose `Color.kt` and Tailwind `index.css`.
* **5. App Store Visual Concept:** 16:9 Google Play Feature Graphic banner (`/public/feature-graphic.jpg`).
